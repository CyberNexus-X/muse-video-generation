# Example — Long-video outline (house format)

> This is a format example with an original, prevention-only outline. Replace the topic for a real video. Do not invent a victim, a name, or a loss figure.

- **Topic:** AI voice cloning scams — kaise pehchanein aur kaise bachein (prevention only)
- **Video type:** Long video, landscape 1920x1080
- **Length:** Full video ~9–10 minutes, 12 scenes, each ~45–55 seconds
- **Language:** Hinglish
- **Style:** Cream editorial — cream canvas + peach wash, black headlines, per-scene accent colours, slide-number pills, white caption pills, animated diagrams

## Scenes (in order)

1. **Hook** — A fake emergency call in a family member's voice. The viewer feels the panic first, then learns it may not be real.
2. **How it works** — How a scam call is put together, in simple steps, without instructions for doing it.
3. **How it works (continued)** — What the scammer wants in the call: urgency, secrecy, and quick payment.
4. **Why it works** — Why our brain trusts a familiar voice, especially in panic.
5. **Red flags (1)** — Urgency, secrecy ("kisi ko mat batana"), and an unusual payment method.
6. **Red flags (2)** — A number you do not recognise, background noise that does not match, and answers that stay vague.
7. **What to do in the moment** — Hang up calmly, call back on the known number, and do not share codes or money on that call.
8. **What to do in the moment (continued)** — Verify through a second channel: a family member, a known number, or in person.
9. **Family protection plan** — A family safe word, and one rule: "paise wali baat par hamesha dobara call karo."
10. **Family protection plan (continued)** — What every family member should know, in one short list.
11. **What NOT to do** — Do not panic-pay, do not share OTPs/codes, do not keep it secret from family.
12. **Closing** — One takeaway: "Awaaz pehchani lage, to bhi verify karo — pehle call back, phir koi kadam."

## Rules for this outline

- Prevention only. No cloning instructions, tool names, or steps for creating a cloned voice.
- No invented case, name, or amount. If a real example is needed, the user must supply it, or it stays general.
- Each scene gets its own animated diagram (call screen, checklist, branching diagram, red-flag cards), not a static slide.
- Karaoke subtitles throughout, scene change every 1–3 seconds in fast-paced sections, SFX/music low under the voiceover.
